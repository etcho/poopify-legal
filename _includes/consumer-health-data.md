This section is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. It is also available as a separate page at [https://etcho.github.io/poopify-legal/consumer-health-data/](https://etcho.github.io/poopify-legal/consumer-health-data/).

- **Consumer health data we collect:** only if you enable Cloud Sync: the health records described in Section 2 (bowel movements, symptoms, pain, medication and food notes, menstrual cycle information, notes), excluding location data. We may also infer that you use a health-related app from analytics identifiers (Section 5).
- **Purpose:** to store and synchronize your records between your devices and restore them, as requested by you.
- **Source:** directly from you, through the App.
- **Sharing:** we do not share consumer health data with third parties or affiliates. It is processed only by our processor, Google Cloud, to host the service.
- **Consent:** we collect consumer health data only with your separate, explicit consent, given in the App before Cloud Sync is enabled. You can revoke it at any time.
- **Your rights:** confirm whether we process your consumer health data, access it, obtain the list of all third parties and affiliates with whom it was shared (none), delete it, and withdraw consent. Exercise them in the App (Section 9.1) or by emailing poopify.dev@gmail.com. You may appeal any decision by replying to our response; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.
- **We never sell consumer health data.**
