# Poopify - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Poopify app (the "App") processes, why, and what rights you have. It applies to the App on all devices and to the optional Cloud Sync service.

## Summary

- **By default, your health records never leave your device.** In Local Mode, everything you record (bowel movements, menstrual cycle, symptoms, notes, profiles) is stored only on your phone. We do not receive it.
- **Cloud Sync is optional.** If you turn it on and give your explicit consent, a copy of your records is stored in the cloud (Google Firebase, in the European Union) so it can be synchronized between your devices. Cloud Sync is being made available gradually and **may not be available in all regions**.
- **We never sell your data, never use your health records for advertising, and never share them with anyone for their own purposes.**
- The App shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting from Google. These services receive device identifiers and app usage information, **but not the content of your health records.**
- You can turn Cloud Sync off, export your data, or delete your cloud account at any time, directly in the App (Menu > Privacy).

## 2. Data stored only on your device (Local Mode)

When you use the App without Cloud Sync, the following data is stored **only on your device**, in the App's private storage:
- the records you enter: date and time, stool characteristics, symptoms, pain, medication and food notes, hygiene method, menstrual cycle information, free-text notes;
- profiles you create (name, icon, optional birth date) and App settings.

We do not have access to this data. It is not sent to us or to any third party, except in the following cases, which you control:
- **Exports and sharing:** when you export a backup, CSV, JSON or PDF, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose to share it.
- **Android device backup:** depending on your device settings, Android may include App data in your device's own backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

## 3. Cloud Sync (optional)

Cloud Sync is an optional feature that is being made available gradually. It **may not be available in all countries or regions**, and where it is not available, the App does not show it.

### 3.1 What we process
If you turn on Cloud Sync, we process:
- **Account data:** a unique account identifier and the email address of the account you sign in with (for example, your Google account), managed by Firebase Authentication.
- **Your health records:** a copy of the records and profiles described in Section 2, **excluding location data**.
- **Technical sync data:** device identifier generated by the App, timestamps of changes and synchronization status.
- **Consent record:** the version of this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use you accepted, and when.
- **App integrity signals:** information that confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of the App (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity).

### 3.2 Why and on what legal basis
- **Purpose:** only to store your records and synchronize them between the devices signed in to your account, and to let you restore them on a new device.
- **Legal basis:** your **explicit consent** (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and art. 9(2)(a); LGPD art. 7, I and art. 11, I; and equivalent rules in other jurisdictions). Account and technical data are processed because they are necessary to provide the service you requested (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V).
- You can **withdraw your consent at any time** by deleting your cloud data in the App (Section 9). Turning off Cloud Sync alone only stops synchronization: the copy already in the cloud stays there until you delete it, and the App offers to delete it when you turn Cloud Sync off. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.
- We **do not** use your cloud data for analytics, advertising, profiling, research, training of artificial intelligence, or any purpose other than providing Cloud Sync to you.

### 3.3 Where your data is stored
Cloud Sync data is stored on Google Cloud servers located in the **European Union** (Firestore multi-region "eur3"). Google acts as our processor under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms.

### 3.4 Who can access it
- **Through the App, only your account.** Security rules on the servers block any other account from reading or writing your data.
- **The developer, only in exceptional cases.** The data is encrypted in transit and at rest, but not end-to-end: technically, it can be seen through the administration console of the service. That console is restricted to the developer account, protected with two-factor authentication, and its access is logged. We look at your cloud data only if you explicitly ask us for help with your account, or if we are required to by law.
- **Google**, as our infrastructure provider, processes the data only to host it on our behalf.

### 3.5 Availability by region
Cloud Sync may be unavailable in some countries or regions for legal or technical reasons. If Cloud Sync becomes unavailable where you are, synchronization stops, your data on your devices is not affected, and you can still delete your cloud account and cloud data at any time in Menu > Privacy.

## 4. Advertising

- The App displays ads provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and information about ads shown and clicked.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where else required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App's menu, under **Ad privacy options** (shown where this choice applies).
- On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- **The content of your health records is never shared with AdMob or used to select ads.**
- If you buy "Remove Ads", ads are no longer shown.
- More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 5. Analytics and crash reports

- We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the App is used (for example, which screens are opened and which features are used), and **Firebase Crashlytics** to receive reports about crashes and errors.
- These services process an App instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from the IP address (country and region), and usage events.
- Usage events record **that** a feature was used (for example, "a record was created"), **never the content** of what you recorded.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX), or your consent where the law requires it.
- Analytics is linked to Google AdMob only to measure the performance and revenue of the ads shown in the App. **Analytics data is not used to personalize ads** (ads personalization and Google signals are turned off in our Analytics settings) and is not shared with other advertising partners.
- **Retention:** analytics event data is kept for 2 months, and user-level analytics data for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

## 6. Purchases

Purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed by **Google Play**. We receive only the information needed to confirm the purchase (such as a purchase token and status). We do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

## 7. Support emails

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message only to respond to you, and we keep the conversation as a record of our support history, until you ask us to delete it. Please do not send health information by email unless necessary.

## 9. Your choices and rights

### 9.1 Available in the App at any time
- **Turn off Cloud Sync:** your data stays on your device and the App keeps working in Local Mode.
- **Delete your cloud account and cloud data:** in the App (Menu > Privacy > Delete a Cloud Sync account, or Menu > Cloud Sync > Delete cloud account and data), or at [https://etcho.github.io/poopify-legal/delete-account/](https://etcho.github.io/poopify-legal/delete-account/).
- **Export your data** (SQL backup, CSV, JSON, PDF), for access and portability.
- **Correct or delete records** directly in the App.

### 9.2 Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; data portability; withdraw consent; not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing; and be informed about with whom data is shared.
- **European Union / EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR):** all rights above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
- **Brazil (LGPD, art. 18):** confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, deletion of data processed with consent, information about sharing, information about the possibility of denying consent and its consequences, and withdrawal of consent. You may also complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).
- **United States:** see Sections 11 and 12.
- **Other countries:** we honor equivalent rights under your local law.

To exercise any right that is not available directly in the App, email **poopify.dev@gmail.com**. We will answer within the period required by law for each type of request (for example, up to 15 days for a complete confirmation of and access to your data under the LGPD, and one month under the GDPR, which the GDPR allows to be extended in some cases). We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

## 13. Children

- **Cloud Sync accounts are only for adults** (18 years or the age of majority where you live).
- Parents and guardians may use the App to keep records about their children, in separate profiles. In that case, the parent or guardian is responsible for that information and consents on the child's behalf.

## 14. Security

We protect your data with technical and organizational measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including:
- encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest;
- server rules that let each account access only its own data;
- verification that requests come from the genuine App;
- restricted, two-factor-protected and logged administrative access;
- data minimization (for example, location data is never synchronized);
- automated tests of our security rules.

No system is completely secure. If a security incident affects your personal data, we will notify the competent authorities and you, when and as required by law (for example, within 72 hours to the supervisory authority under GDPR).
